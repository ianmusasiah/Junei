Important timeline information:

These are illustrative planning ranges, not official processing guarantees. Some stages can overlap, and actual timelines depend on admission deadlines, scholarship decisions, university requirements, visa appointment availability and programme duration.

The Malaysian route does not automatically transfer you to a US university. You must apply separately and qualify for admission and an F-1 visa.

Completing KCSE keeps both pathways open. Do not leave secondary school for an unconfirmed scholarship.
